## What this covers

- The target-language columns (`Target (KO)`, `Target (FR)`, …) of
  `wsr_package_build/locales/<locale>/WSR_translation_*.csv`
- The translated values in `wsr_package_build/locales/<locale>/vars/` and
  `wsr_package_build/locales/<locale>/glossary.csv`
- The runtime files generated from them
  (`wsr_package_build/game_files/js/locale/<prefix>-*.json`)

## What this does not cover

- The English source text (`Source (EN)` columns, `source_data/`,
  `reference_data/`) and every other part of Wall Street Raider. These belong
  to their respective rights holders and are included only so translators
  can work against them.
- The project's code, which is licensed under the PolyForm Noncommercial
  License 1.0.0 (see `LICENSE`).
